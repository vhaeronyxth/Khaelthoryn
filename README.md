# Khaelthoryn

> The mirror was carried into the forest face down. The trees began remembering the sky.

## The Forest without an Outer Edge

Khaelthoryn appears in travelers' journals as a forest of dark trunks and pale roots. Its boundary cannot be approached from outside. A traveler discovers the first tree only after finding a leaf inside a closed pocket.

The paths curve toward a clearing whose shape changes with the weather beyond the forest. During rain, the clearing is round. During frost, it has seven corners. Nobody has described its shape in summer, though several journals contain pages smelling faintly of warm fruit.

At the center stands a covered mirror. Its frame is carved from horn, and its surface faces the earth. The cloth covering its back bears an image of the trees, drawn from a height no bird has reached.

## The Antlered Messenger

A messenger visits the clearing whenever a root turns silver. It walks on four legs, carries folded wings beneath its shoulders, and wears antlers whose branches end in small closed eyes.

The messenger brings no letters. It places one hoof beside the covered mirror and waits until the trees stop casting shadows. Then a single eye opens upon its antlers, showing a landscape different from the clearing.

Some witnesses see a frozen lake. Others see a narrow tower surrounded by sunlit grass. One traveler saw the room where he had left his sleeping child, except that the child was awake and watching the forest through a mirror laid across the floor.

The messenger closed the eye before the traveler could call a name.

## The Bargain of the Seven Corners

During a winter of unusual silence, seven travelers met in the clearing. Each had found a leaf in a pocket. Each believed the leaf belonged to a tree that had been cut down long before.

They offered the leaves to the messenger. It accepted six, placing them beneath the mirror without lifting its frame. The seventh leaf remained in the final traveler's palm, becoming transparent whenever he tried to release it.

The messenger lowered its antlers. One closed eye rested against the leaf. The traveler heard a voice ask what he had brought instead of a memory.

He answered with his own name. The leaf fell. Around the clearing, the seven corners became six, and the journals ceased mentioning how many travelers had arrived.

## Instructions Found in a Hollow Trunk

The hollow trunk contains a small wooden box, a silver needle, and a length of thread that casts the shadow of a branch. Inside the lid, four instructions have been burned into the wood.

> Sew only what the mirror has forgotten.
>
> Leave the final eye closed.
>
> If the thread becomes warm, turn toward the tree that has no leaf.
>
> Never ask the root which side of the earth it remembers.

A traveler followed the first instruction by stitching a tear in the mirror's cloth. His stitches appeared across the sky reflected in a puddle nearby. At sunset, those stitches loosened, and an unfamiliar star descended between them.

He placed the needle back inside the box. The thread remained warm throughout the winter.

## The Language of the Silver Root

**Khael — thor — yn — aevis.**

These sounds are heard where a silver root enters the soil. They resemble words when spoken slowly and the beating of wings when repeated without a pause.

The forest's watchers refuse to repeat them. They carve four empty spaces into a strip of bark instead, allowing the wind to decide what belongs between the marks. The strips are hung beside the mirror, where they remain still even during storms.

One strip began moving on a windless morning. Its marks filled with the name of the seventh traveler. By evening, the letters had become small roots reaching toward the cloth. The watchers buried the strip beside the hollow trunk.

The following winter, the clearing had seven corners again.

## The Witness beneath the Cloth

The mirror has never been turned upright. Nevertheless, travelers sometimes see faces in its covered back: their own, a stranger's, or the messenger's antlers reflected in water.

A watcher placed a hand against one of those faces. Something beneath the cloth placed a hand against his. Its fingers were longer, ending in claws that followed the shape of the frame without touching it.

The watcher withdrew. For several days, his palm reflected a forest seen from above. He kept his hand closed until the image faded. When he opened it again, a small leaf lay upon his skin, silver on one side and dark on the other.

## Where the Last Tree Waits

The watchers keep the mirror covered. The box remains inside the hollow trunk, and the needle has acquired a fine layer of frost that does not melt in a living hand.

The messenger returns when a root turns silver. Its final eye remains closed. Sometimes its antlers cast the shadow of an eighth branch, although nobody can find that branch upon its head.

Khaelthoryn offers a path to anyone who carries an unfamiliar leaf. The traveler who finds the last tree may discover why the sky continues remembering stitches that nobody placed above it.
